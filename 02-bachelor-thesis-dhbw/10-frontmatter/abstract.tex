\acl{RAG} systems in highly regulated domains such as German tax law depend on the quality of their embedding-based retrieval stage, yet standard commercial embedding models suffer from a well-documented domain gap when applied to specialised legal text. 
The lexical density, morphological complexity and fine-grained semantic distinctions of German tax law induce geometric pathologies in general-purpose embedding spaces ...
